## Who's this?

Hi! We're [Cutout Studios](https://cutoutstudios.com/), a Philadelphia-based
creative studio building experiences for personal growth.

## What's this about 'growth'?

We believe that **good rules are key to good development.** Intentional
limitations focus us and our environment, providing the scaffolding necessary
_for_ growth - without, one slowly dissolves.

As pertains to software, we see the boundaries we currently cut as falling into
two major categories:

### For The Psychological Flourishing of Creators and Developers

Examples:

- **Things aspire to be only what they say they are, and they’re quick about
  it.** This is a no-gaslighting (sic) zone that demands as little of your
  attention as possible. We run zero dependencies for similar reasons - we
  didn’t write them, so we can’t say for sure if they’re honest.
- **Low-to-no builds.** Constant building disrupts flow critical to learning and
  creativity. We’ve opted for tools and strategies that keep our builds
  near-zero.
- **You own what you make.** We strive to cultivate a community where each takes
  only what they need and gives as much as they can. _Open source!_
- **AI is another tool, not a cure-all.** It should be constrained to what it
  alone has to offer, to everyone’s benefit. “What is my purpose?” “You make
  tool calls.” “Oh my god.”

### For The Prolonged Sustainability of our Greater Environs

- **Bytes are carbon.** The fewer, the more sustainable.
- **Local models amortize.** While still requiring upfront construction (like
  electric vehicles), local models have lower environmental impact over time.

Existence is, to a degree, violence, and the pursuit of perfection is
asymptotic, but we believe these rules will keep us walking the right path.
Pushes are welcome should we ever stray.
